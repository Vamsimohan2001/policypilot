# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Purpose:** This policy explains how hybrid and remote working operates: eligibility, in-office expectations, working hours, equipment, security, and expenses. It applies to all staff whose role is designated hybrid or remote in the HR system.

## Eligibility and Work Models

Roles are designated as one of three models: office-based, hybrid, or fully remote. Hybrid employees are expected to work from their assigned office at least 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday as company-wide anchor days when teams should be on site. Fully remote employees must attend any mandatory onsite weeks announced at least 4 weeks in advance. Employees on a performance improvement plan or within their first 60 days of onboarding are expected to work onsite unless their manager approves otherwise in writing.

## Working Hours and Availability

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible provided the employee completes a standard 40-hour week. Employees must keep their calendar current and respond to urgent channels within 2 hours during their working day. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours whenever a participant is in a different time zone.

## Security Requirements

When working outside the office, employees must connect through the company VPN on any untrusted network, keep devices encrypted and screen-locked, and avoid processing confidential data in public spaces where screens are visible. Working from a country outside the employee's registered location for more than 30 cumulative days per year requires prior approval from HR and the security team.

## Equipment and Home Office

The company provides a laptop and, for remote employees, a one-time home-office allowance of 500 US dollars for a desk, chair, or monitor. Purchases should be made through the approved vendor portal and are company property. Employees are responsible for reasonable care of equipment and must report loss or damage within 4 hours under the IT Acceptable Use and Device Policy.

## Expenses and Travel

Commuting to the assigned office is not reimbursable. Approved business travel, including team onsite weeks, is booked through the travel platform. Internet reimbursement of 30 US dollars per month is available to fully remote employees on submission of a receipt.

## Attendance and Performance

Remote work does not change performance expectations. Repeated unavailability during core hours, missed anchor days, or declining output are handled through normal performance management. Persistent refusal to meet onsite expectations may result in a change of work model at the company's discretion.
