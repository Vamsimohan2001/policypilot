# Compensation and Benefits Policy

**Purpose:** This policy documents the salary structure, review cycle, bonus framework, equity, insurance, parental benefits, and retirement contributions. It is restricted to HR staff because it contains confidential compensation mechanics.

## Salary Bands and Structure

Base pay is organised into five bands, B1 through B5, with B1 for entry-level roles and B5 for directors. Each band has a midpoint and a range of plus or minus 15 percent around the midpoint. Offers must be made within band unless a written exception is approved by the Chief People Officer. Bands are benchmarked against the market every 12 months using the annual compensation survey.

## Salary Review Cycle

The annual salary review takes effect on 1 April. Managers submit recommendations in February, calibration completes in March, and letters are issued by 20 March. Off-cycle adjustments are reserved for promotions, significant scope changes, or retention cases and require CPO approval. Promotions move employees a minimum of one step within the target band.

## Performance Bonus

Eligible employees participate in an annual performance bonus of 10 to 20 percent of base salary, depending on band and individual rating. Target bonus percentage is 10 percent for bands B1-B2, 15 percent for B3-B4, and 20 percent for B5. Actual payout scales from 0 to 1.5 times target based on company results (60 percent weight) and individual performance (40 percent weight). Bonuses are paid with the April payroll following the review cycle.

## Equity

Employees in band B3 and above receive an equity grant at hire and are eligible for refresh grants every two years based on performance. Grants vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. HR administers grants through the equity platform and coordinates with Finance on the cap table.

## Insurance and Wellbeing

The company provides health insurance covering the employee and dependants at no premium cost, with an annual coverage limit of 25,000 US dollars and a no-cashless facility at network hospitals. Term life insurance of 3 times annual base salary and a 24/7 employee assistance programme are included. Annual health check-ups are reimbursed up to 150 US dollars.

## Parental and Family Benefits

Parental leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, available to all employees after 6 months of service. Employees may extend unpaid for up to 12 additional weeks. A phased return with 60 percent working hours at full pay applies for the first 4 weeks after returning.

## Retirement and Separation

The company contributes to the statutory retirement scheme as required by local law and adds a voluntary matching contribution of up to 6 percent of base salary. Separation pay, notice periods, and leave encashment follow the employment contract and local statutory requirements.

## Confidentiality

Compensation details are strictly confidential. HR staff may access them only for administration and must never disclose individual compensation outside approved processes such as calibration.
