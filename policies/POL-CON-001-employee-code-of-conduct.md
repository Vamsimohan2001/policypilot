# Employee Code of Conduct

**Purpose:** This policy defines the standards of professional behaviour expected from every member of staff, regardless of role, seniority, or location. It applies to all employees, contractors, and interns and is a condition of employment.

## Core Principles

All staff must act with integrity, honesty, and professionalism. Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect, keep commitments, and avoid any behaviour that could damage the trust placed in the company. Decisions must be made in the company's best interest and never for personal gain.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to their manager and to compliance within 5 business days of becoming aware of it. Typical examples include hiring or managing a relative, holding a financial interest in a supplier, or taking secondary employment with a competitor or customer. Disclosure is reviewed confidentially and does not automatically disqualify the situation.

## Gifts, Hospitality, and Bribery

Employees may accept or offer gifts only when they are of nominal value, meaning a market value of 75 US dollars or less per occasion, are not cash or cash equivalents, and could not reasonably be seen as an attempt to influence a business decision. Anything above this threshold must be declined politely or, if refusing would cause offence, declared to compliance within 3 business days. Bribery and facilitation payments are strictly prohibited anywhere in the world, including through third parties and agents.

## Confidentiality and Company Assets

Company information, customer data, pricing, roadmaps, and unreleased products are confidential. Employees must not share them externally without written authorisation, and the obligation continues after employment ends. Company equipment and systems must be used primarily for business purposes in line with the IT Acceptable Use Policy.

## Respectful Workplace

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation are not tolerated. This includes conduct based on race, gender, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. Complaints are handled confidentially under the Grievance and Disciplinary Policy, which HR administers.

## Reporting Violations

Suspected violations can be reported to a manager, to HR, or through the confidential compliance mailbox. Reports may be made anonymously. The company prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith; retaliation is itself a disciplinary offence.

## Consequences

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, and where appropriate, referral to law-enforcement authorities. Managers must apply this policy consistently and fairly across their teams.
