# IT Acceptable Use and Device Policy

**Purpose:** This policy governs the acceptable use of company computers, mobile devices, accounts, networks, and software, and sets out device-security requirements and incident-reporting duties.

## Acceptable Use

Company systems are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is tolerated when it is lawful, brief, and does not interfere with work or consume significant resources. Prohibited uses include: accessing illegal content; installing unlicensed software; using company systems for personal business or crypto-mining; sending harassing or discriminatory material; and attempting to bypass security controls, including endpoint agents, content filters, or authentication.

## Approved Software and AI Tools

Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed on company devices. New tools require an IT review request; review target is 5 business days. Generative-AI tools must be used exclusively through the company-approved AI platform. Confidential, personal, or customer data must never be pasted into unapproved AI tools, browser extensions, or external chatbots.

## Device Security Requirements

All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (FileVault or BitLocker), automatic screen lock after 5 minutes, and the company endpoint-protection agent running and up to date. Operating-system updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Mobile devices accessing company email must use a device passcode and enrol in mobile device management. Jailbroken or rooted devices are prohibited from accessing company data.

## Removable Media and Data Transfer

Use of USB storage devices requires prior IT approval and must use company-issued encrypted drives. Transferring confidential data to personal cloud accounts is prohibited. Data exports containing personal data require a documented business justification approved by the data protection officer.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 4 hours of discovery so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. If the device may have held customer data, the incident is escalated as a security incident under the Incident Response Policy. Delayed reporting that worsens exposure is itself a policy violation.

## Accounts and Credentials

Employees must never share passwords, MFA codes, or tokens, including with IT staff. The service desk will never ask for a password. Suspected account compromise must be reported immediately so credentials can be rotated.

## Monitoring and Privacy

The company may monitor system usage, network traffic, and device compliance for security and operational purposes, consistent with local law. Monitoring is not used to covertly track productivity metrics of individuals.

## Consequences

Violations may result in suspension of access, disciplinary action, and in serious cases termination and legal referral. Minor breaches are handled with coaching and remediation.
