# Leave and Attendance Policy

**Purpose:** This policy sets out all leave entitlements, how leave is accrued and requested, approval rules, sick leave, public holidays, and attendance expectations.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees accrue 2 days of paid annual leave per completed month of service, up to a maximum of 24 days per calendar year. Employees who join mid-year receive a pro-rated entitlement. Annual leave accrues from the first day of employment but may only be taken after completing the 90-day probation period unless the manager grants an exception in writing.

## Carry-Over and Encashment

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March, after which they lapse. Encashment of annual leave is not permitted except at separation, where accrued and unused days are paid out at the base daily rate.

## Requesting Leave

Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 consecutive days, and at least 4 weeks in advance for absences longer than 10 consecutive days. The reporting manager approves or declines leave within 3 business days; silence is not approval. Team members should coordinate so that no more than one third of a team is on leave at the same time during critical periods.

## Sick Leave and Medical Absence

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, accruing at 1 day per month. Absences of up to 2 consecutive days require only notification to the manager on the morning of the first day. Absences of 3 or more consecutive days require a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal. Sick leave cannot be used as substitute annual leave. Long-term medical absence beyond 10 days is handled under the illness and disability provisions administered by HR.

## Public Holidays and Office Closures

The company observes 12 public holidays per year, published in the HR portal by 31 January. Where a regional office holiday differs, employees follow the holiday calendar of their registered work location. If a statutory holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute day is granted unless local law requires it.

## Attendance Expectations

Employees must be available during core collaboration hours, notify their manager of any unplanned absence by 10:00 local time, and keep time-tracking records accurate where applicable. Persistent unapproved absence or repeated lateness is addressed through the performance improvement process after a documented conversation with HR support.

## Special Leave

Bereavement leave of up to 5 paid days is available for immediate family. Jury duty and civic obligations are paid. Parental leave entitlements are defined in the Compensation and Benefits Policy.
